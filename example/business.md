# Me and my business (an example with representative numbers, not a real client)

- Me: 34, founder of a performance marketing agency in Austin, 5 years in. I run every sales call myself.
- The agency: paid ads and creative for e-commerce brands. 18 clients at about $3,000 a month ($54,000 a month). Team of
  8: me, 2 account managers, 4 media buyers and designers, 1 ops. Margin fell from 31% to 16% in two years: salaries and
  tools went up, prices did not, and clients want more reporting.
- The account managers: 2 at $4,500 a month each, all in. About 60% of their week is weekly reports, status updates,
  client emails and meeting notes. The rest is client calls and keeping clients calm when results dip.
- Churn: about 1 client leaves a month. 2 of the last 5 who left said "communication" (slow replies, late reports).
- The AI idea: a studio quoted $14,000 and 6 weeks to build an AI system that drafts the weekly reports, status updates
  and client emails from the ad accounts, plus $600 a month to run. Then I let both account managers go (a month of
  severance each, $9,000) and the media buyers take the client calls. That saves about $8,400 a month.
- The sales idea: hire a closer at $5,000 a month plus 10% of each new client's first-year fees, about 3 months to ramp.
  I get 20 to 25 sales calls a month from referrals and LinkedIn, close about 2 in 10, and push half of them because I
  am in delivery. A new client is worth about $3,000 a month.
- Cash: $70,000 in the bank, about 6 weeks of costs. No debt.
- The rule I set: this quarter the money backs one of the two, or neither. Both is out: not the cash and not the
  headspace. Neither means no build, no firing and no hire.
- Leaning: replace the account managers with AI. Everyone says AI does their job now, and I am tired of paying people to
  write reports.
