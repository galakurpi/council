# Brief: replace account managers with AI, or hire a salesperson?

**The decision.** The money this quarter backs one option, or neither. Both is out.
- A: Replace AMs with AI
- B: Hire a closer
- C: Neither, keep the cash

**The business.** Performance marketing agency, Austin, 5 years. 18 e-commerce clients at about $3,000/mo = $54,000/mo. Team of 8: founder, 2 account managers (AMs), 4 media buyers/designers, 1 ops. Margin fell from 31% to 16% in two years (salaries and tools up, prices flat, more reporting asked for). Leads: 20 to 25 sales calls a month from referrals and LinkedIn; founder closes about 2 in 10 and pushes half of them because he is in delivery. A new client is worth about $3,000/mo.

**The cash.** $70,000 in the bank, about 6 weeks of costs, no debt.
- A: $14,000 build (6 weeks) + $600/mo, plus $9,000 severance. Saves about $8,400/mo ($9,000 AM cost less $600). Media buyers take client calls.
- B: Closer at $5,000/mo + 10% of each new client's first-year fees, about 3 months to ramp.

**The AMs.** $4,500/mo each, all in. About 60% of their week is reports, status updates, emails, meeting notes. The rest is client calls and calming clients when results dip. Churn is about 1 client a month; 2 of the last 5 leavers said "communication".

**Founder leaning.** A, replace the AMs with AI: "Everyone says AI does their job now, and I am tired of paying people to write reports." The council tests this lean.

**Assumed:** the studio's quote and 6-week timeline are accurate (assumed); a closer's close rate matches the founder's 2 in 10 (assumed).
