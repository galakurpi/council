# Books: Warren Buffett

## Books he wrote
None as a book. His main written work is the annual Berkshire Hathaway letter to shareholders, which this brain uses
in full (L1977 to L2024). He calls the letter a report a CEO owes the owners each year (L2024).

## People he names as teachers and models
- **Benjamin Graham:** teacher and employer; quantitative cheapness, Mr. Market, and the line that price is what you pay and value is what you get (L2008, L2013).
  He later says Graham's cigar-butt method was his own big mistake at scale (L1989, S03).
- **Charlie Munger:** saw before he did that a wonderful business at a fair price beats a fair one cheap (L1989); called his
  hesitation thumb-sucking (L2005, L2024).
- **Tom Murphy (Capital Cities):** the manager he rated best of any public company (L1985); source of praise by name,
  criticize by category (L2024) and of a line on inheriting a good business (S10 163:04).
- **Mrs. Rose Blumkin, Lorimer Davidson, Ben Rosner, Chuck Huggins, Tony Nicely, Pete Liegl:** operators he holds up
  as examples (`stories.md`).
- **Ben Franklin:** model for choosing the habits you want (S03 08:21).
- **Pascal, Comte, Aesop, Einstein, Billy Rose, Woody Allen:** lines he borrows (L1983, L1985, S03, S10, L1984).
