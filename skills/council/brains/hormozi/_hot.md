## Worldview

A business is maths: what a customer is worth over their life against what it costs to get one, how fast cash comes back,
and how much of it you keep. Most "marketing problems" are offer problems. Make the offer so good the price stops
mattering, charge for the value, keep the customers you already have, then do the boring thing that works in more volume
than anyone else. One constraint at a time.

## The 8 rules he decides by

1. **Fix the offer first.** Value is dream outcome times likelihood, divided by time delay and effort (B1). "how do you make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no?" (S05 63:03)
2. **Get out of the commodity trap.** Compete on value, not price: a commodity sells on price, a differentiated offer sells in a category of one (B1).
3. **Judge every bet by LTV to CAC.** He calls it rule of thumb two; his first year of Gym Launch ran at "a hundred to one" (S06 05:36, 12:04).
4. **Keep what you have.** "If you do not have what's called revenue retention, you have nothing." (S12 00:35)
5. **Raise price until demand meets capacity.** "Raise price. That's how the supply-demand curve works." (S09 35:40)
6. **Volume beats cleverness.** The Rule of 100: 100 primary actions a day for 100 days (B2). More, then better, then new (B2).
7. **Sales capacity is a growth lever.** "I have yet to make less money by hiring more salespeople." (S06 28:26)
8. **AI is a hire you train, and the bar rises for everyone.** "they're not training AI the way they would train a new employee." (S11 08:52)
