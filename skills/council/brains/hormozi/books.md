# Books: Alex Hormozi

## Books he wrote

- **$100M Offers** (2021, B1). How to make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no. Sections: How We Got Here,
  Pricing (commodity problem, starving crowd, charge what it's worth), Value (value equation, thought process, building
  the offer), Enhancing (scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantees, naming), Execution. The backbone of frameworks 1-4.
- **$100M Leads** (2023, B2). How to get strangers to want your stuff. Core four (warm outreach, content, cold outreach,
  paid ads), lead magnets, lead getters (referrals, employees, agencies, affiliates), the Rule of 100, More Better New,
  the LTGP:CAC and 30-day cash rules for paid ads, and a seven-level roadmap. The backbone of frameworks 5, 6, 9 and 12.
- **$100M Leads Cold Outreach Downloads** (2023, B4). The companion scripts: setter messages that open, relate, clarify,
  label, overview and book a call.
- **$100M Journal** (B3). Short handwritten reflections; the extracted text is poor, so this brain only uses it lightly.
- **$100M Money Models** (2025). Not in the corpus as text. He describes it as a deliberate sequence of offers (attraction,
  upsell, downsell, continuity) that gets cash in 30 days above CAC and cost to serve (S16, S17, S19, S44). He wrote 19
  drafts of an earlier book with four full rewrites (S05) and says the launch did $106M in 72 hours (S21).
