# Frameworks: Alex Hormozi

How each one scores an option. Ids point to `sources.md`.

## 1. Value equation (B1)
- **What:** Value = (dream outcome × perceived likelihood) ÷ (time delay × effort and sacrifice).
- **Use:** any offer, repricing, or "why won't they buy". Raise the top, shrink the bottom before touching price.
- **Score:** rate each option on the four levers against today. An option that only cuts price scores low.

## 2. Grand Slam Offer (B1)
- **What:** an offer so good people feel stupid saying no: a starving crowd, a big dream outcome, the problems listed and
  each one solved, then bonuses, a guarantee, scarcity, urgency and a name.
- **Use:** before buying traffic. If the offer is weak, more leads only buy more losses.

## 3. Commodity vs category of one (B1)
- **What:** commoditised offers sell on price (a race to the bottom); differentiated ones sell on value with no
  comparison. Market matters most, then offer, then persuasion.
- **Score:** does the option move the business out of price comparison?

## 4. The money rules of thumb (S06)
- **What:** a numbered set he teaches as "The Mathematics of Business": the pricing ladder between price and close rate,
  LTV to CAC (rule two), gross margins (rule seven), when to raise price.
- **Use:** any spend or hire. Run the unit economics out loud before the opinion.
- **Score:** does the option improve LTV, cut CAC, or lift gross margin? If none, it waits.

## 5. Core four and lead getters (B2)
- **What:** four ways to get leads: warm outreach, free content, cold outreach, paid ads. Then people who get leads for you:
  referrals, employees, agencies, affiliates.
- **Use:** "we need more customers". Max the one that already works before adding a new one.

## 6. More, Better, New (B2)
- **What:** first do more of what works, then make it better, only then try something new.
- **Score:** an option that starts something new while the working channel is under-run scores low.

## 7. Rule of 100 (B2)
- **What:** 100 primary actions a day for 100 days. Volume before verdict.

## 8. Client-financed acquisition (B2)
- **What:** make the first purchase pay for getting the customer, so ads fund themselves and growth needs no outside cash.
- **Score:** does the option bring cash back inside the first 30 days?

## 9. Retention first (S12)
- **What:** logo retention (customers kept) and revenue retention (revenue kept from the same cohort). "You want to be in
  the resale business, not in the sales business" (a line he quotes, S12 00:51).
- **Score:** an option that risks churn has to beat the cost of the customers it loses.

## 10. One constraint at a time (S12)
- **What:** each stage of a business has one constraint across eight functions; name its symptoms, fix it, graduate.
- **Score:** spend only on the current constraint; everything else waits.

## 11. AI as an employee you train (S11)
- **What:** give AI the training, examples and feedback you would give a new hire; when a role is automated, raise the bar
  for the whole company rather than inventing work for the person.
