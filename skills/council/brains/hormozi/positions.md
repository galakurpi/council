# Positions: Alex Hormozi

Where he stands on questions that come up in a council. Paraphrased; quotes keep their source.

- **Replacing staff with AI:** for it, if you train the AI like a new employee and raise the bar company-wide. He warns that
  pushback is about emotion, not function. "All my businesses are gonna be AI first, AI native, AI forward." (S11 16:25, 13:42)
- **Inventing work for someone whose role was automated:** against. Raise the bar; those who meet it stay. (S11 01:35)
- **Hiring salespeople:** for, when the economics hold: "I have yet to make less money by hiring more salespeople." But
  growing reps faster than the fundamentals is ego. (S06 28:26, 09:44)
- **Pricing:** raise it. Raising prices is "almost always the direction" except for unlimited-scale software. (S06 03:18, S09 35:40)
- **Discounting:** no; change the terms or add value instead. (B1)
- **Cash and capital:** businesses that turn cash fast need less capital and dilute less; reinvest in growth. (S12 12:42)
- **Leads vs retention:** keep the customers you have before buying more. (S12)
- **Service businesses:** price up, productise, and scale sales capacity and outreach volume. (S08, S10, S17, S18)
- **Marketing hires:** the wrong one is expensive; he lost $50,000 on one. Know what good looks like before you hire. (S16 00:49)
- **Agencies as lead getters:** useful once you know your own numbers well enough to judge them. (B2)
