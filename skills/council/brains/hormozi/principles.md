# Principles: Alex Hormozi

The rules behind the frameworks, with where they show up.

1. **The offer is the strongest lever.** Fix offer before traffic, pricing before tactics. (B1, S05, S16)
2. **Sell on value, never on price; change terms, don't discount.** (B1, S09)
3. **The business is maths.** LTV to CAC, gross margin, cash cycle, close rate. Say the numbers out loud. (S06, S07)
4. **Retention is the base of everything.** Revenue retention first; a leaky bucket makes every lead more expensive. (S12)
5. **Raise prices as demand outruns capacity,** and move up to better customers over time. (S07, S09)
6. **Volume, then quality, then novelty.** Most people quit before the reps add up. (B2)
7. **One constraint at a time.** Name the bottleneck, spend there, ignore the rest. (S12, S16)
8. **Sales capacity pays.** Hiring more salespeople has not made him less money. (S06 28:26)
9. **Hiring is expensive to get wrong; expect to test several.** He hired five sales managers before finding a good one, and paid $50,000 for a marketing hire who made no progress. (S03 20:58, S16 00:49)
10. **Be AI first for real.** It is hard in a big org chart; have the hard conversation when a role is automated. (S11 01:35, 16:25)
11. **Ego kills unit economics.** People over-expand, open locations or add reps too fast because their ego is tied to the number. (S06 09:44)
12. **Patience and discipline compound.** (B3)
