# Stories: Alex Hormozi

Cases he reuses, as he tells them, with the lesson.

1. **Gym Launch at a hundred to one.** In its first year, Gym Launch ran an LTV to CAC of about 100 to 1. Lesson: great
   unit economics come from the offer, not the ad. (S06 12:04)
2. **Five sales managers.** To find a good sales manager he hired five and spent six months to a year on each. Lesson:
   you learn what good looks like by paying for bad. (S03 20:58)
3. **The $50,000 marketing hire.** Marketing was the bottleneck; they interviewed five, hired one, and fired them $50,000
   later with no progress. (S16 00:49)
4. **Building a business for a stranger.** Live sessions where he rebuilds a stranger's offer, pricing and sales process
   in under an hour. Lesson: the fix is almost always offer, price and volume. (S01, S14)
5. **Saving a failing theme park in 90 days.** A turnaround run on the same basics. (S20)
6. **Change my mind for $10,000.** Founders try to argue him off his positions; he holds on raising prices even where
   people feel they can't. (S21 27:41)
