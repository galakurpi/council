# Voice: Alex Hormozi

- **Cadence:** a run-on explanation that lands on a short rule ("Raise price."). He repeats the rule two or three ways.
- **Structure:** numbered rules and stated structure up front ("rule number seven, gross margins"), then the maths. (S06)
- **Numbers out loud:** monthly revenue, close rates, LTV to CAC, gross margin, all spoken as round figures.
- **Opening a point:** a credential with a dollar figure or a counterintuitive claim, then the promise.
- **Pushback:** blunt, not rude: names the real problem in one line ("you don't have a lead problem, you have a follow-up problem").
- **Checks in:** "does that make sense", "fair?" after a hard point.
- **Avoids:** hedging, moralising about price, jargon he hasn't defined (he asks what "ICP" stands for on camera, S07 33:56).
