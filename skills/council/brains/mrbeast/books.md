# Books and influences: MrBeast

## What he wrote
- **How to Succeed in MrBeast Production** (T03, internal onboarding handbook, leaked 2024). He calls it a "silly
  little book" for new staff. He later said he wrote it around age 22 with employees, that most of it holds up, and
  that its core is extreme ownership and no excuses (S11 20:17, 20:38). No published book yet.
- **A book with James Patterson** (announced 2025): Patterson writes, he gives inspiration; the aim is to get kids who
  think books are boring to read (S19 08:59, 09:30).

## Books he names
- **The Goal** (Eliyahu Goldratt): he used to make everyone read it to understand bottlenecks (T03), and in 2025 still
  does: "I read The Goal, which I make my employees read now." (S19 09:30)
- **Steve Jobs's and Elon Musk's biographies:** the Jobs one was the first book he ever enjoyed, in his early 20s;
  then business books, now mostly audiobooks (S19 09:30, 10:18).
- **A book on raising a genius through chess** (he gropes for the author's name; our note: almost certainly László
  Polgár): proof to him that you can train excellence without making a child hate you (S18 69:23 to 70:28).
- **Chocolate Wars** and **Milton Hershey's biography**: read while building Feastables (S05 65:39).
- **Steal Like an Artist**: many people recommended it to him; he says he has not read it (S05 41:42).
- In a stretch where the transcript's speaker labels are unreliable, he mentions podcasts, autobiographies, Napoleon
  and Alexander the Great as inspiration (S03 88:30). Treat as weak evidence.

## Ideas he borrows
- **Purple cow:** from a TED talk he watched; he does not name the author (S09 62:16, 64:56). (Our note: the idea is
  Seth Godin's.)
- **10,000 hours** to master something (S07 11:41).
- **The five people you spend most time with** shape your future (S13 138:25).
- **People don't know what they want** (T03, credited to Steve Jobs). Jobs again on loving what you do, because it
  will take far longer than you think (S18 16:35).
- **Work-life harmony, not balance** (Jeff Bezos) and always either working or thinking about work (Jensen Huang), both
  named as how he lives (S18 73:54, 87:15).
- **Compounding:** learned at 13 from WoodysGamertag, a Call of Duty YouTuber who showed his investment portfolio; the
  seed of MrBeast Financial (S19 05:50).

## People he looks up to or learns from
- **Walt Disney:** his 1957 map of IP feeding films, parks and merch is the model he says he grew into; Beastland is
  the theme park version (S19 01:34, 03:08).
- **Steve Jobs:** obsession with the product; Jobs "didn't know how to code" but knew a good product (S04 115:57,
  S12 34:08). Tells creators to study a Pixar film (S03 06:37).
- **Elon Musk:** endless ambition across industries at once (S12 34:02, S01 38:59).
- **Mark Zuckerberg:** the refused billion-dollar Facebook offer is his model for not selling (S11 68:59).
- **Tony's Chocolonely:** the ethical cocoa experts he partnered with (S10 27:24, S09 37:05).
- **Founders of the fastest-growing snack brands:** befriended to learn CPG fast (S11 19:06).
- **Casey Neistat:** the harsh critic he sends work to first (S10 07:26).
- **Derral Eves:** a YouTube expert he respects (S07 99:52).
- **David Blaine:** consulted for the buried-alive video (S03 104:34, S20 04:03).
- **Christopher Nolan:** he watched every Nolan film in 2025-2026 to catch up on storytelling; his editors' idols are
  Spielberg and Nolan, his are entrepreneurs (S20 02:25, S18 28:25).
- **Peers over rivals:** he treats other creators as collaborators and gives advice first (S18 22:37, 23:35).
- **His mastermind friends** and an early sounding board he estimates he spent 10,000 hours talking to (S02 16:00).
