## Notes on reliability
- **S14, S17, S21** are multi-camera vlogs with many speakers and unstable labels: his lines sit under several labels
  (S14 mostly S1; S17 under S0, S3, S17, S27; S21 under S0 and, as host, S7). Only lines that are clearly his are
  quoted; anything said by his directors, crew or contestants is left out.
- **S19** is shared with Mark Rober; only Jimmy's answers (label S0) are used.
- **S08** is Colin and Samir's own compilation. Jimmy appears only in one short clip (36:24 to 37:03), labelled as a
  host by the transcript; it is used once.
- **S03** has stretches where host lines are merged into Jimmy's (around 62 to 66 min and 83 to 91 min); nothing is
  quoted from them except one flagged mention in `books.md`.
- **S07** was published on YouTube in 2024 but he says he is 23 in it, so it was recorded earlier.
- **T02** is not the full memo: only the sentences AP quoted as his.
- Some swear words are dropped from the S02 transcript.
