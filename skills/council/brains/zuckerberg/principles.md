# Principles: Mark Zuckerberg

The rules behind the frameworks, with where they show up. Quotes are his.

1. **Reach goes to content, not to follower counts.** By early 2023, AI recommendations from unfollowed accounts were over a fifth of the Facebook and Instagram feed and about 40% of Instagram, and had driven time spent on Instagram up more than 24% (T04, T06). Recommendation gains kept adding time spent every quarter since (T11, T13, T15). (S05, S08)
2. **Make what people will be glad they saw.** A long-term company optimizes for "what people are gonna find valuable over time, not what is gonna draw people's attention today" (S03 45:03). (S10)
3. **Feed is moving toward the best content, and best includes socially relevant.** "Feed, I think, is gonna continue moving largely in the direction of just the best content" (S08 08:37). Sharing into group chats is the social act now (S08, T06 on Reels reshares).
4. **The system targets better than you do.** "Eventually we got to the point where our ads system could better predict who would be interested than the advertisers could themselves" (T09). (S11, T11)
5. **Creative is the lever left to the advertiser, and AI is taking that too.** Generated creative is "especially valuable for smaller advertisers with limited budgets" (T13). (T19, S11, S04)
6. **Pay for outcomes.** He wants more Meta products priced so businesses pay only for results (T19), and frames ads as a results machine (S11, T11).
7. **Messaging is where small business is going.** "Businesses often tell us that more people open their messages and they get better results on WhatsApp than other channels" (T04). Business agents reached 1 million businesses a week (T19). (T11, T15, S11, S04)
8. **Learn faster than everyone.** Ship early, get data, iterate (S06, S02, T01, T07). "hackers would rather just prototype something and see what works" (T01).
9. **Ship before it is polished, but not before it is useful.** "you're almost at the line of being embarrassed about what you put out" (S06 26:32), yet bad releases teach nothing because nobody uses them (S06).
10. **Not taking risks guarantees failure in a fast market.** "in a world that's changing so quickly, you're guaranteed to fail if you don't take any risks" (T01). (S02)
11. **Expect mistakes; learn fast and keep going.** "don't even bother trying to avoid mistakes because you're gonna make tons of mistakes" (S01 17:34). Good calls often come from not repeating an earlier mess (S05).
12. **Focus on one or two things.** (S01, S05, T01)
13. **Flatter is faster, leaner is better.** (T05, S04, T04, T06, T16, T17) "A leaner org will execute its highest priorities faster." (T05)
14. **Layoffs are a last resort, and the leader owns the error.** "I view layoffs as a last resort, so we decided to rein in other sources of cost before letting teammates go" (T03). "I got this wrong, and I take responsibility for that." (T03)
15. **People over org charts.** "That's like, you can't hire that. You have to build that." (S11 36:05), on a team with long trust. He allocates the best people first, then money (S11).
16. **AI amplifies people.** "People will be more important in the future, not less." (T17) (S10, S11, S12, T14, S07)
17. **Profit buys the right to take long bets.** Survive and get self-sustaining first (S11); the efficiency memo says profit is what funds innovation (T05). (T09, T15)
18. **Learn from the market and copy what works.** "There are more smart people outside of your company than inside your company." (S06 31:57) (S01 on Lockdown)
19. **Start with a problem, not with a company.** "you should start with the problem that you're trying to solve in the world and not start with, um, deciding that you want to build a company" (S02 04:37). (S06, S01)
20. **Values show in trade-offs.** "values are not what you write down on the wall. It's like your lived behaviors" (S06 09:13).
